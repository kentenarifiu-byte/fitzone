Start where you are

Workout ideas

Choose an activity that feels right for you. Take breaks when you need them and focus on learning good movement.

Beginner

Full-body basics

A gentle introduction to basic movements such as squats to a chair, wall push-ups and easy marching.

At home

No-equipment movement

Try a relaxed routine with mobility movements, step-touches and simple balance practice.

Recovery

Stretch & reset

Take time for comfortable stretches and easy movement after a busy day. Never force a stretch.

Start gently, use a clear space and stop if something hurts or makes you feel unwell. Ask a qualified adult or health professional for advice if you are unsure what activity is suitable.

About us

Fitness without the pressure.

FitZone is a welcoming place for people with different experience levels. Our goal is to make movement feel approachable, enjoyable and part of everyday life.

You do not need to be an expert to begin. Small, consistent habits can help you build confidence over time.
